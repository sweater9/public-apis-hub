# Bitcoin Halving

Approximately every 210,000 blocks (~4 years), Bitcoin’s block subsidy halves. This slows new supply issuance and is a core part of Bitcoin’s monetary schedule toward the 21 million cap. Halvings are protocol rules, not promises about price.
