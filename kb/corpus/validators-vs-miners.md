# Validators vs Miners

**Miners** (PoW) spend energy and hardware to win the right to produce blocks. **Validators** (PoS) lock stake and run consensus software to propose/attest blocks.

Both can be offline (miss rewards) or malicious (risk of orphaned work or slashing). Operators must keep software updated, monitor peers, and secure keys. Home users can participate on some networks; others favor professional operators due to hardware or capital requirements.
