# BaaS and Neobanks

- **Neobank** — digital-first retail bank or bank-like app (often partnering with a licensed bank rather than holding a full license itself)
- **BaaS (Banking-as-a-Service)** — licensed banks expose ledger, KYC, accounts, cards, and payments via APIs so fintechs can embed banking features
- **Sponsor bank / partner bank** — the licensed institution standing behind a fintech program
- **Program manager** — operates the consumer brand, support, and product UX while the bank holds the regulatory relationship

Risk for institutions: third-party oversight, BSA/AML accountability, and operational resilience when fintech partners fail.
