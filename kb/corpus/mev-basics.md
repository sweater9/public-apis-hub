# What Is MEV?

Maximal (or Miner/Validator) Extractable Value is profit from reordering, inserting, or censoring transactions. Sandwich attacks on DEX swaps are a common example. Users can reduce risk with tighter slippage, private orderflow where available, and avoiding huge swaps in public mempools without protection.
