# FX and Treasury Basics

Corporate and bank treasury vocabulary:
- **Spot FX** — near-immediate exchange of two currencies
- **Forward / swap** — contracted future exchange; used to hedge
- **Nostro / vostro** — correspondent balances held for cross-border settlement
- **Liquidity management** — ensuring cash is available across entities and currencies
- **Payment factory** — centralized processing of outbound payments

Crypto treasury desks add **stablecoin inventory**, on-chain settlement windows, and gas/fee planning to this classic toolkit.
