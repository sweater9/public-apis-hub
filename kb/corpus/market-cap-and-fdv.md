# Market Cap and Fully Diluted Valuation

**Market cap** ≈ price × circulating supply. **FDV** ≈ price × max/total supply if all tokens were circulating. A low market cap with huge unlocks can still mean heavy future dilution.

Also watch token emissions, team allocations, and vesting. Price alone does not tell you if a network is "cheap."
