# Ethereum Fees and EIP-1559

EIP-1559 introduced a **base fee** that is burned plus an optional **priority fee** tip to validators. Wallets estimate fees; users can adjust. Burning base fees ties fee pressure partly to ETH scarcity dynamics, separate from staking issuance.
