# ISO 20022 Messaging

**ISO 20022** is a global standard for richer financial messaging (XML/JSON data models). Payment systems and SWIFT are migrating from older MT messages to **MX** ISO 20022 formats.

Why institutions care:
- Better structured remittance data
- Improved screening and reconciliation
- Cross-border payments reform (e.g. G20 roadmap)

Treasury and payments teams talk about **pain.001** (customer credit transfer initiation), **pacs.008** (FI-to-FI customer credit transfer), and related message types.
