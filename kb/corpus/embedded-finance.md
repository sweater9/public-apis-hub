# Embedded Finance

**Embedded finance** means offering financial products inside a non-bank customer journey (e-commerce checkout, ride-share app, vertical SaaS).

Examples: instant payouts to sellers, **BNPL**, branded cards, invoice financing, embedded insurance.

The brand owns UX; a licensed partner owns the regulated activity. Institutions evaluate these as **distribution partnerships** with shared compliance obligations.
