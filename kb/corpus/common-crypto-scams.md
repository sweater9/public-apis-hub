# Common Crypto Scams

Frequent patterns:
- **Seed phrase phishing** — fake "wallet sync", airdrop claim, or support sites
- **Approval drainers** — malicious token approvals after connecting a wallet
- **Pig butchering / romance investment scams** — fake profits then withdrawal fees
- **Rug pulls** — teams abandon liquidity or mint infinite tokens
- **Fake apps and extensions** — malware wallets
- **Giveaway scams** — "send ETH, get double back"

Rule of thumb: real support never needs your seed. If urgency and secrecy are pushed, pause.
