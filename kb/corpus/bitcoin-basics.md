# Bitcoin Basics

Bitcoin (BTC) is the first widely adopted cryptocurrency, launched in 2009. It is a peer-to-peer electronic cash system with a hard-coded supply approaching **21 million** coins. New coins are issued as block rewards to miners who secure the network with Proof of Work.

Important ideas:
- **UTXO model** — wallets track unspent outputs rather than simple account balances
- **Halving** — roughly every four years the block reward halves, slowing issuance
- **Confirmations** — waiting for several blocks reduces double-spend risk
- **Self-custody** — controlling your seed phrase means you control the coins; losing it means losing funds

Bitcoin is primarily used as a store of value and settlement layer. It has limited smart-contract capability compared with Ethereum; more complex apps often use other chains or Layer 2 systems built around Bitcoin.
