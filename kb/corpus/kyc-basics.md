# KYC Basics

**KYC (Know Your Customer)** is the process financial institutions use to identify and verify who a customer is — and, for companies, who owns and controls them — before and during a business relationship. KYC is a core part of wider anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing programs.

Typical KYC elements (high level):
- Collect identity data (name, date of birth, address, nationality; for entities: legal form, registration)
- Verify with reliable documents or electronic sources
- Identify beneficial owners for legal persons
- Screen against sanctions and PEP lists
- Assign a risk rating that drives due-diligence depth and refresh frequency

This corpus describes what institutions **typically require** and how regimes differ in outline. It is **not** legal advice and does **not** explain how to circumvent controls.
