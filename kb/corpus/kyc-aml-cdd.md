# KYC, AML, and CDD

Financial institutions must know their customers and monitor for financial crime.

- **KYC (Know Your Customer)** — collecting and verifying identity (legal name, address, ID documents, beneficial owners for companies)
- **CDD (Customer Due Diligence)** — risk-based checks at onboarding and periodically thereafter
- **EDD (Enhanced Due Diligence)** — deeper review for higher-risk customers (PEPs, high-risk countries, complex ownership)
- **AML (Anti-Money Laundering)** — policies, transaction monitoring, and reporting to stop laundering of illicit funds
- **SAR / STR** — suspicious activity (or transaction) reports filed with the national FIU when red flags appear
- **Sanctions screening** — checking names against lists (OFAC, UN, EU, local) before and during the relationship

Banks, EMIs, crypto exchanges, and payment firms all run variants of this stack. Failures lead to fines, license risk, and frozen accounts.
