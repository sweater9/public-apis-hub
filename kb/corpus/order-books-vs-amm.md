# Order Books vs AMMs

Centralized and some on-chain venues match bids and asks in an order book. AMMs quote prices from pool reserves. Order books can be capital-efficient for market makers; AMMs are always-on and simple for long-tail tokens.

Hybrid designs exist. Choose based on size, pair liquidity, and whether you need self-custody settlement.
