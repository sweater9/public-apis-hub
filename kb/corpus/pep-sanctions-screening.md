# PEPs and Sanctions Screening

- **PEP (Politically Exposed Person)** — someone with prominent public function (plus often family/close associates); higher corruption risk → EDD
- **Sanctions lists** — OFAC SDN and others; matching can be exact or fuzzy; false positives are common
- **True match / false positive** — analysts clear alerts; true matches may require blocking and reporting
- **Ongoing screening** — re-check customers as lists update, not only at onboarding

Institutions buy screening data vendors and tune match thresholds; over-tuning creates either missed hits or unmanageable alert volumes.
