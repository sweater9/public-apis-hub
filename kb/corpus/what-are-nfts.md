# What Are NFTs?

Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) are unique on-chain tokens (often ERC-721 or ERC-1155 on Ethereum). They can represent art, memberships, game items, or certificates. Ownership of the token is on-chain; media files may live on IPFS or centralized servers.

NFTs do not automatically grant copyright unless a license says so. Markets are speculative; wash trading and fake collections are common. Verify collection contracts and creator authenticity.
