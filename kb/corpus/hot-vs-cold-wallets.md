# Hot Wallets vs Cold Wallets

**Hot wallets** stay online (mobile apps, browser extensions, exchange accounts). They are good for spending money and interacting with dapps, but malware, phishing, and supply-chain attacks are bigger risks.

**Cold wallets** keep signing keys offline. Hardware devices and carefully air-gapped setups reduce remote theft risk. You still must verify receive addresses on the device screen and protect the seed.

A common approach: keep a small working balance hot, and cold-store long-term holdings. Multisig cold setups add resilience against a single lost or compromised device.
