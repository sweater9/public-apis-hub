# Cross-Chain Bridges Explained

Bridges move value or messages between chains. Designs include lock-and-mint, burn-and-mint, and liquidity networks. Bridges have been frequent exploit targets because they concentrate large treasuries and complex trust assumptions.

Prefer canonical/official bridges for large amounts, verify destination addresses and chains, and consider the security model (multisig, light clients, optimistic verification). Speed often trades off against safety.
