# Open Banking and PSD2

**Open banking** lets licensed third parties access account data or initiate payments with customer consent, usually via bank APIs.

In the EU/UK, **PSD2** created roles such as:
- **AISP** — Account Information Service Provider (read balances/transactions)
- **PISP** — Payment Initiation Service Provider (start a payment from the user’s bank account)
- **ASPSP** — the account-holding bank that must expose APIs

Strong Customer Authentication (SCA) and consent management are core. Similar regimes exist or are emerging elsewhere (e.g. CDR in Australia, open finance initiatives). Institutions talk about **account aggregation**, **variable recurring payments**, and **embedded finance** built on these APIs.
