# Stablecoins Overview

Stablecoins are crypto tokens designed to track a reference (usually USD). Major types:
- **Fiat-backed** — reserves of cash/T-bills held by an issuer (e.g. USDT, USDC styles)
- **Crypto-collateralized** — overcollateralized on-chain (e.g. DAI-style systems)
- **Algorithmic** — try to maintain peg with supply adjustments; historically fragile

Stablecoins enable trading pairs and DeFi collateral but carry issuer, regulatory, depeg, and smart-contract risks. "Stable" is a design goal, not a guarantee.
