# Public Keys vs Private Keys

Cryptocurrencies rely on public-key cryptography:
- **Private key** — secret; used to sign transactions
- **Public key** — can be shared; used to verify signatures
- **Address** — a shorter identifier derived from the public key (format varies by chain)

Sharing your address is normal (to receive funds). Sharing your private key or seed is never safe. If a site or "support agent" asks for your seed, it is a scam.
