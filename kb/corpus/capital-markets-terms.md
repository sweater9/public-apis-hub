# Capital Markets Terms for Fintech

Terms that appear when fintech meets markets:
- **AUM** — assets under management
- **NAV** — net asset value of a fund share
- **Prime brokerage** — bundled custody, financing, and execution for funds
- **Market making** — providing continuous bid/ask liquidity
- **OTC** — over-the-counter trades bilaterally negotiated, common for large crypto blocks
- **RFQ** — request for quote

Institutional crypto desks borrow this vocabulary when describing liquidity, financing, and client coverage models.
