# Correspondent Banking

**Correspondent banking** lets a bank provide account services to another bank, enabling cross-border payments where direct access is missing.

- **Correspondent** — the bank providing the account
- **Respondent** — the bank using that account
- **Intermediary / cover payments** — additional banks in the chain
- **De-risking** — exiting respondent relationships perceived as high risk

Cross-border crypto and fintech flows often still touch correspondent rails when converting to fiat. ISO 20022 and AML expectations on correspondent chains have tightened.
