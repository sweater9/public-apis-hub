# MSB, EMI, and Licensing Terms

Common regulated labels:
- **MSB (Money Services Business)** — US category covering money transmission, exchange, checks, etc.; FinCEN registration plus state money-transmitter licenses often required
- **EMI (Electronic Money Institution)** — EU/UK license to issue e-money and provide payment services
- **PI (Payment Institution)** — payment services without issuing e-money
- **Broker-dealer / ATS** — securities trading intermediaries
- **VASP / CASP** — virtual asset service provider terms under FATF and regional crypto regimes (e.g. EU MiCA CASP)

Product teams map features (custody, exchange, staking, lending) to these licenses; wrong mapping is a classic compliance failure mode.
