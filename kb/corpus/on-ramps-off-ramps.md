# On-Ramps and Off-Ramps

- **On-ramp** — converting fiat to crypto (card, bank transfer, Apple Pay, etc.)
- **Off-ramp** — converting crypto back to fiat in a bank account

Institutions care about **funding sources**, chargebacks (cards), return rates (ACH), KYC tiering, and which legal entity is the money transmitter. Banking partners may restrict certain MCC codes or high-risk geographies. OTC desks serve large tickets with RFQ pricing instead of retail app flows.
