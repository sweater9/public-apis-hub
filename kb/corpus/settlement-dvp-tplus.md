# Settlement, DvP, and T+ Cycles

- **Trade date (T)** — when the trade is agreed
- **Settlement date (T+n)** — when securities and cash finally exchange; US equities moved toward **T+1**
- **Clearing** — novation and netting via a CCP before settlement
- **CSD** — Central Securities Depository holding securities in book-entry form
- **DvP (Delivery versus Payment)** — securities move if and only if cash moves, reducing principal risk
- **PvP** — payment-versus-payment for FX, reducing Herstatt risk

Tokenization projects often pitch **atomic settlement** (on-chain DvP) to compress these cycles.
