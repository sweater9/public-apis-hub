# Layer 2 Scaling Overview

Layer 2 (L2) systems process transactions off the base chain (Layer 1) while inheriting security assumptions from it. Popular Ethereum L2s use **rollups**: execute off-chain, post data/proofs to L1.

Benefits: lower fees, higher throughput. Tradeoffs: bridge delays/risks, sequencer centralization in early stages, and different tooling. Always use official bridges and confirm you are on the intended network in your wallet.
