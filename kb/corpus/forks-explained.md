# Hard Forks and Soft Forks

A **soft fork** tightens rules in a backward-compatible way. A **hard fork** introduces rules that old software rejects, potentially splitting the chain if communities disagree. Users should follow reputable client teams during upgrades and beware of replay risks on contentious splits.
