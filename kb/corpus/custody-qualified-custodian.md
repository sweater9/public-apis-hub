# Custody and Qualified Custodians

**Custody** means safekeeping of assets on behalf of clients.

- **Omnibus wallet** — many clients’ assets under one chain address with internal ledgers (operationally simple; bankruptcy remoteness depends on structure)
- **Segregated custody** — client assets separated per client or per wallet
- **Qualified custodian** — in several securities regimes, an eligible bank/trust/broker that may hold client securities and meet capital/control standards
- **Cold storage / MPC / HSM** — technical controls for key management in digital-asset custody

Banks entering crypto often start with **custody and brokerage** rather than on-chain trading desks. Insurance, SOC reports, and key ceremonies are part of institutional diligence language.
