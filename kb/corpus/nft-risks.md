# NFT Risks and Scams

Common NFT pitfalls: phishing mints, fake support Discord DMs, malicious setApprovalForAll prompts, royalties confusion, and illiquid markets. Blind signing in wallets can approve dangerous contract calls.

Protect yourself: bookmark official sites, ignore unsolicited mint links, revoke approvals after sales, and treat floor-price screenshots as marketing, not guarantees.
