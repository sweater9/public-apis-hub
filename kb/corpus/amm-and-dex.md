# AMMs and Decentralized Exchanges

Automated Market Makers (AMMs) let users trade against a liquidity pool instead of a traditional order book. Liquidity providers (LPs) deposit token pairs (or concentrated ranges) and earn fees, but can suffer **impermanent loss** if prices move.

Spotters swap by sending tokens into the pool; prices follow a formula (e.g. constant product x * y = k). Always set slippage limits, watch for MEV sandwich risk on public mempools, and verify you are on the real frontend and correct chain.
