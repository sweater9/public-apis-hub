# Proof of Stake (PoS)

Proof of Stake selects block proposers and attesters based on staked coins rather than hashpower. Validators lock capital; dishonest behavior can be **slashed** (stake destroyed or confiscated by protocol rules).

Pros: lower energy use than PoW, economic penalties for attacks, often faster slot times. Cons: wealth concentration concerns, more complex client software, need careful design against long-range and nothing-at-stake issues.

Ethereum, many Cosmos chains, Solana (variant), and others use PoS or related designs. Staking can be solo, via a pool, or through liquid staking tokens — each with different risks.
