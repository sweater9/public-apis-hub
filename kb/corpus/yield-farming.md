# Yield Farming Basics

Yield farming means moving capital across DeFi protocols to earn trading fees, interest, and incentive tokens. High advertised APYs often come from inflationary reward tokens and can collapse quickly.

Risks: contract exploits, rug pulls, temporary APY mirages, gas costs that erase gains, and complex tax reporting. Prefer understanding the source of yield over chasing the largest number on a dashboard.
