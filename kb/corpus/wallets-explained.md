# Crypto Wallets Explained

A crypto wallet does not "store coins" the way a physical wallet stores cash. It stores **keys** that authorize spending on the blockchain. The network holds the balances; your keys prove control.

Types:
- **Hot wallets** — software connected to the internet (convenient, higher attack surface)
- **Cold / hardware wallets** — keys stay offline for signing (safer for larger amounts)
- **Custodial wallets** — an exchange or app holds keys for you (easier recovery, counterparty risk)
- **Multisig** — several keys must sign before funds move

A **seed phrase** (usually 12 or 24 words) can recreate the private keys. Anyone with the seed can steal funds. Never type a seed into a website, screenshot it, or share it in chat. Back it up offline, and test recovery with a tiny amount first.
