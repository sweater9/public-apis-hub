# Centralized vs Decentralized Exchanges

**CEX** (Coinbase, Binance-style): custodial accounts, fiat on-ramps, deep liquidity, KYC. Risk: exchange hacks, freezes, insolvency.

**DEX**: non-custodial swaps via smart contracts. Risk: contract bugs, MEV, UX mistakes, thinner liquidity for some pairs.

Many users deposit fiat on a CEX, withdraw to self-custody, then use DEXs. "Not your keys, not your coins" applies whenever an exchange holds balances.
