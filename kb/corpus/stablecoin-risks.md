# Stablecoin Risks and Depegs

A depeg happens when a stablecoin trades away from its target (e.g. $0.90 instead of $1.00). Causes include reserve doubts, bank failures affecting issuers, smart-contract bugs, liquidity crunches, or failed algorithmic designs.

Mitigations for users: diversify stablecoin exposure, prefer transparent attestations/audits, understand redemption mechanics, and avoid assuming any coin cannot fail.
