# DeFi Lending and Borrowing

On-chain lending protocols let users supply assets to earn interest and borrow against collateral. Positions are typically overcollateralized. If collateral value falls, **liquidation** can sell collateral to repay debt.

Watch borrow rates, collateral factors, oracle design, and governance risk. Stablecoin depegs and cascading liquidations have caused major losses historically.
