# Self-Custody Best Practices

Self-custody means you control keys. Practice receiving and sending small amounts, verify addresses on hardware screens, keep redundant offline backups in separate locations, and document recovery for heirs without exposing secrets insecurely. Diversify across wallet brands carefully — do not casually import the same seed into many apps.
