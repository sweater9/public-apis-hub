# Card Issuing and Acquiring

Card products split into two sides:

- **Issuing** — the bank or fintech that gives the cardholder a card and account; earns interchange; manages credit/debit risk and fraud
- **Acquiring** — the bank/PSP that enables a merchant to accept cards; routes transactions to schemes; settles to the merchant (minus fees)

**Schemes** (Visa, Mastercard, etc.) set network rules. **Interchange** is the fee paid toward the issuer. **Scheme fees** go to the network. The merchant discount rate (MDR) bundles these plus acquirer margin.

BIN sponsorship and **Banking-as-a-Service** let fintechs issue cards under a licensed bank’s sponsorship.
