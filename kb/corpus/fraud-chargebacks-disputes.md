# Fraud, Chargebacks, and Disputes

Payments risk vocabulary:
- **Authorization** — issuer approves a card payment
- **3-D Secure (3DS)** — extra cardholder authentication step
- **Chargeback** — cardholder disputes a charge; merchant may lose funds and pay fees
- **Friendly fraud** — legitimate customer disputes a real purchase
- **Velocity limits / device fingerprinting / rules engines** — common controls

Crypto ramps inherit card dispute risk; bank transfers shift risk toward returns, recalls, and APP (authorized push payment) scams depending on market.
