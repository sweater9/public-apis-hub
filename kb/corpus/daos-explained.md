# DAOs Explained

A Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) coordinates people with on-chain rules and treasury control, often via governance tokens. Members propose and vote on upgrades, grants, or parameter changes.

Challenges: low turnout, whale dominance, legal ambiguity, and operational security of multisigs. Holding a governance token is not the same as equity in a traditional company.
