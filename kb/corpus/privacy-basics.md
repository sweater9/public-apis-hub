# Privacy Basics in Crypto

Most major chains are pseudonymous, not anonymous: address activity is public. Reusing addresses and withdrawing from KYC exchanges to personal wallets can link identities. OpSec includes fresh addresses carefully used, avoiding doxxing your holdings on social media, and understanding that mixers may be legally restricted in some places.
