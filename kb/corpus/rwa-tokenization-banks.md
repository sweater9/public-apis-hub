# RWA Tokenization for Institutions

**RWA (Real-World Asset) tokenization** means representing traditional assets (funds, deposits, bonds, treasuries) as digital tokens.

Bank and asset-manager language:
- **Permissioned networks** — only KYC’d participants
- **Atomic DvP** — tokenized cash vs tokenized security
- **Deposit tokens** — bank liabilities on-chain (distinct from independent stablecoins)
- **Transfer restrictions** — whitelists, lockups, jurisdiction controls

Legal wrappers (fund shares, notes) and custody of the underlying remain as important as the smart contracts.
