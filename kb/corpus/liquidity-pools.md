# Liquidity Pools Explained

A liquidity pool holds reserves that traders swap against. LPs receive pool tokens representing their share. Fees accrue to LPs according to the pool’s rules.

Risks: impermanent loss, smart-contract bugs, and (in some designs) opportunistic rebalancing. Concentrated liquidity can earn more fees but needs active management. Read the protocol docs before depositing.
