# CBDCs vs Cryptocurrencies

Central Bank Digital Currencies are digital forms of government money, typically permissioned and issued by monetary authorities. Public cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin are usually decentralized bearer assets with different privacy, custody, and censorship properties. They solve different problems and should not be conflated.
