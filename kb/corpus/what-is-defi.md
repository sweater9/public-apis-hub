# What Is DeFi?

Decentralized Finance (DeFi) refers to financial apps built mainly on smart-contract platforms. Common products include decentralized exchanges (DEXs), lending markets, stablecoin systems, yield aggregators, and derivatives.

DeFi aims to be open and composable: protocols can plug into each other. Risks include smart-contract bugs, oracle failures, liquidation cascades, admin-key abuse, and phishing when approving tokens. Always check contract addresses, revoke unused approvals, and never invest more than you can afford to lose.
