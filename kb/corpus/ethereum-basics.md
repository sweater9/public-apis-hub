# Ethereum Basics

Ethereum is a programmable blockchain. Besides transferring ETH, it runs **smart contracts** — programs stored on-chain that execute when called. That enables tokens (ERC-20), NFTs (ERC-721), DeFi protocols, DAOs, and more.

ETH is used to pay **gas** (transaction fees). After the Merge (2022), Ethereum secures itself with **Proof of Stake** instead of mining. Account balances are tracked in an account model (unlike Bitcoin's UTXOs).

Users interact through wallets (e.g. browser extensions or hardware devices) that sign transactions. Always verify contract addresses and permissions; malicious approvals can drain tokens even if your seed phrase stays secret.
