# Banking Basics

Banking is the business of taking deposits, making loans, and providing payment and related financial services under a banking license and prudential regulation. Banks intermediate savings into credit, operate payment accounts, and manage liquidity and credit risk on a balance sheet supervised by a central bank or banking authority.

Core activities typically include:
- **Deposit taking** — current/checking, savings, and term deposits
- **Lending** — retail, mortgage, and corporate credit
- **Payments** — domestic and cross-border transfers, cards, cash management
- **Treasury** — funding, FX, and interest-rate management for the bank itself and clients

This article is a general industry overview for education — not advice on any specific bank or jurisdiction.
