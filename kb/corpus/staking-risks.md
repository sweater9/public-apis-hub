# Staking Risks Summary

Staking can earn protocol rewards but exposes you to slashing, downtime, smart-contract risk (liquid staking), custody risk (exchange staking), liquidity discounts, and opportunity cost if you need cash quickly. Diversify methods only if you understand each trust model. Never stake funds you cannot leave locked under protocol rules.
