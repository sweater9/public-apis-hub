# Trade Finance Terms

Trade finance products finance import/export flows:
- **Letter of Credit (LC)** — bank undertakes to pay if documents comply
- **Documentary collection** — banks handle documents without the same payment undertaking as an LC
- **Bill of lading / invoice / packing list** — key shipping documents
- **Supply-chain finance / reverse factoring** — early payment to suppliers against buyer credit

Blockchain pilots often target document authenticity, status tracking, and reducing discrepancies — institutions still care about UCP rules and correspondent networks.
