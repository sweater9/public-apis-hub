# AML Basics

**AML (anti-money laundering)** refers to laws, regulations, and firm programs designed to detect and deter the disguise of criminal proceeds as legitimate funds. Obliged institutions (banks, many payment firms, brokers, and others) maintain risk-based AML programs.

Common program pillars:
- Policies, governance, and a nominated compliance officer / MLRO
- Customer due diligence (KYC/CDD/EDD)
- Transaction monitoring and sanctions screening
- Suspicious activity / transaction reporting to the national FIU
- Training, independent testing, and record-keeping

Requirements differ across Middle East, US, UK, EU, and APAC, though FATF standards influence most frameworks. Educational overview only.
