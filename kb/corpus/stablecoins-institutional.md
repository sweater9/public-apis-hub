# Institutional Stablecoins

**Stablecoins** are tokens designed to maintain a stable value, usually pegged to a fiat currency (often USD).

Institutional vocabulary:
- **Reserves** — cash, T-bills, or other assets backing the token
- **Attestation / audit** — third-party reports on reserve composition
- **Mint / redeem** — creating tokens against fiat in, or burning tokens for fiat out (often via regulated partners)
- **On-shore vs offshore issuance** — licensing and banking access differ by jurisdiction
- **Travel Rule / AML** — transfers above thresholds may require originator/beneficiary info between VASPs

Banks discuss stablecoins as **settlement assets**, **treasury tools**, or **deposit alternatives**, with focus on redeemability, reserve quality, and counterparty risk of the issuer.
