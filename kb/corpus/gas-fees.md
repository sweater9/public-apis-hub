# Gas Fees Explained

Gas measures computational work on networks like Ethereum. You pay **gas used × gas price** (plus base fee / priority fee mechanics depending on the fee market). Congestion raises prices.

Tips: use L2s for smaller payments, avoid peak hours, set priority fees thoughtfully, and simulate transactions. Failed transactions can still cost gas because validation work was done.
